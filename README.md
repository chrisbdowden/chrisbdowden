# Christopher B. Dowden

Founder & CEO, [@pulselogicbio](https://github.com/pulselogicbio).

I work on computational methods for biomarker discovery — deterministic, tuning-free feature selection in high-dimensional biological data, with validation across oncology and neurodegenerative disease cohorts.

---

## Research focus

* **Methodology.** Stability-governed feature selection for high-dimensional transcriptomics; reproducibility frameworks for biological ML pipelines.
* **Validation.** Disease-agnostic biomarker discovery across oncology, Alzheimer's disease, and Parkinson's disease cohorts.
* **Reproducibility.** Deterministic execution, frozen cross-validation splits, audit-ready workflows.

## Open questions

Active lines of inquiry I am exploring and would welcome feedback on:

1. **Prodromal detection.** Can pre-motor neurodegenerative signatures be reliably detected in prospective cohorts using deterministic, tuning-free feature selection?
2. **Multi-modal fusion.** What is the right architecture for combining genomic, transcriptomic, and methylation signals in a disclosure-discipline-respecting way?
3. **Single-cell scaling.** Can stability-governed feature selection extend to single-cell and spatial transcriptomic settings without overfitting?
4. **Clinical utility.** What measurement frameworks best capture biomarker translation from cohort-level discovery to patient outcomes?
5. **Generalization.** How do biomarkers discovered in one population transfer to others?

If you work on related questions, I would value the conversation: [ceo@pulselogic.bio](mailto:ceo@pulselogic.bio).
